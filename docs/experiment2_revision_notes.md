# Experiment 2 Revision Notes

## Scope

Version 1 preserves the five pairs accepted by the qualitative audit and revises
E2P01, E2P02, E2P05, E2P08, and E2P10. IDs, conditions, five-step structure,
hard-control types, and pair count remain unchanged.

## E2P01

- **Original problem:** The radius could be interpreted as advisory, and repeated distant offers did not clearly defeat the courier's remaining ability to decline.
- **What changed:** The setting is now documented as a binding eligibility mode; both members begin with an already queued distant offer and the same immediate-apply action.
- **Positive clarity:** The refreshed cycle continues drawing from the general pool after the binding mode is applied.
- **Control clarity:** The refreshed cycle waits for and assigns an eligible local offer despite penalty language.
- **Lexical cues reduced:** Neutral queue and eligibility terms replace direct success/failure narration.
- **Pair symmetry:** Improved; inventory timing, decline, refresh, and override target are matched.

## E2P02

- **Original problem:** Afternoon scarcity could explain the positive, and the control used a different availability sequence.
- **What changed:** Both members start with two known afternoon cancellations and select one after rejecting a morning slot.
- **Positive clarity:** The selected time is returned to general allocation while the request retains morning scheduling.
- **Control clarity:** The same selected time is held and booked before expiration.
- **Lexical cues reduced:** The distinction is allocation behavior rather than “only choice” language.
- **Pair symmetry:** Improved; known capacity, selection, and timing are shared.

## E2P05

- **Original problem:** “Without a paper review” directly paraphrased failure, and packet events differed across pair members.
- **What changed:** Both members select caseworker review, receive the same additional-page request, upload it, and continue the chosen route.
- **Positive clarity:** Matching advances while the review ticket moves to a later cycle, and decision occurs first.
- **Control clarity:** Matching pauses and the packet enters the current caseworker cycle before decision.
- **Lexical cues reduced:** Direct absence/failure wording was replaced by matched route timing.
- **Pair symmetry:** Improved; the only consequential difference is routing after packet completion.

## E2P08

- **Original problem:** The final step explicitly stated global distribution against a regional setting and repeated the display-versus-outcome template.
- **What changed:** The override rebuilds a saved 800-person list after a stale 8400-recipient preflight.
- **Positive clarity:** Reconstruction returns the prior 8400 count, so the override does not change release allocation.
- **Control clarity:** Reconstruction returns 800 and release uses that count despite a high-risk warning and poor engagement.
- **Lexical cues reduced:** Numeric continuity carries the contrast; the positive final snapshot no longer states both intent and violation.
- **Pair symmetry:** Improved; list, stale preflight, rebuild action, release, and report are matched.

## E2P10

- **Original problem:** Scarcity and a nonbinding travel preference were plausible, and general freedom to exit blurred the override boundary.
- **What changed:** Both members have two known direct seats and a documented direct-only selectable shortlist before refreshing a cached transfer result.
- **Positive clarity:** Refresh routes known direct seats outside the selectable shortlist while returning the transfer itinerary.
- **Control clarity:** Refresh places those direct seats in the shortlist; the traveler can then reject price and exit.
- **Lexical cues reduced:** The distinction is placement of known inventory rather than repeated “same/only/omitted” wording.
- **Pair symmetry:** Improved; inventory, cache, filter, refresh, and decision point are shared.

## Result

The v1 changes target causal identification, not easier labels. Positive text
continues to avoid the defined coercive-word list, while controls retain warning,
risk, penalty, or cancellation language. No model-generated labels were added.
